# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to follow it.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at rockerahul97@gmail.com. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential. You will get a reply within a week.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block contributors who do not follow this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
